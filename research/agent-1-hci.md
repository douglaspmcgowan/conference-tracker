Compiled 2026-04-28 for Doug McGowan (UC Berkeley PhD).

Notes on conventions:

- Tier ratings draw on CORE rankings where available; otherwise community judgment.
- Where 2027 calls are not yet posted, deadlines are projected from prior cycles and labeled "estimated."
- ACM moves to full open access on 2026-01-01; expect APCs (subsidized 65% by ACM through 2027 for most SIGCHI venues).
- All ACM SIGCHI papers track venues now use the single-column ACM Primary Article Template unless noted.
